I study how the brain forms sensory representations and how these are affected by motor and cognitive processes. My focus is on the visual system and how movements can alter how we perceive visual information. In my research, I use electrophysiology alongside rich behavioral measurements and neural encoding models to deconstruct neural activity into meaningful and interpretable components.
