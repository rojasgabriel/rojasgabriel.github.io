### Overview

I study sensory representations and how they can be altered by motor and cognitive processes. My focus is on the visual system and how movements can alter how we perceive visual information. In my research, I use electrophysiology alongside rich behavioral measurements and neural encoding models to deconstruct neural activity into meaningful and interpretable components.

I use Neuropixels probes to record activity from many neurons at once in primary visual cortex (V1). I combine these recordings with measurements of behavior and statistical models to ask how walking and other movements relate to the way neurons represent visual cues, and whether those relationships vary with task engagement.
